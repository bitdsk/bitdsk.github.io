esp32 How to install an antenna?

2025-01-20

First replace the esp resistor position.
Replace the resistor in position 1 to position 2.
The red box is the position of 1.
The blue box is the position of 2.

Step 2: Carefully insert your antenna into the antenna port. Be careful not to break it.
