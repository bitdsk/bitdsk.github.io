Two ways to update

OTA or full reflash

Which one you need depends on what state the device is in.

↻

OTA upgrade (normal)

Download the .zip for your model, open the miner's web UI, go to Settings → Firmware and upload it. Settings and CD KEY are preserved. This is the right choice 95% of the time.

Find your build →
⚡

Full erase & reflash

For a bricked board, a wiped CD KEY, or a device that will not boot. Needs a USB cable and Chrome/Edge — the flasher runs entirely in the browser using the local image files.

Open flash tool →
⚠

Before you start

Back up your CD KEY from Settings → System. If the key is lost it cannot be recovered — a backup is the only way to restore a device after a full erase.
